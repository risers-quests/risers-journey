# Grade 4 syllabus (the school's maths textbook)

Transcribed from the chapter openers ("Learning outcomes" and "Let's recall")
of the Grade 4 book. This is the spec for the Grade 4 diagnostic: every
Grade 4 question should test one of these outcomes, at this level, in this
book's words and number style.

**Number style:** Indian place-value grouping (9,99,999 — lakhs), rupees and
paise. Pictographs use a key with a scale ("Each ★ stands for 2 points").

| # | Chapter | After the chapter, children can… | Recall at the start (Grade 3 level) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Large Numbers | write number names of 5- and 6-digit numbers; find place value and face value; compare numbers up to 9,99,999; form the largest and smallest numbers from 5 or 6 digits; round to the nearest ten, hundred, thousand; explain the rules for Roman numerals | numbers up to 10 000; 10 ones = 1 ten … 10 thousands = 1 ten thousand |
| 2 | Addition and Subtraction | add and subtract up to 6-digit numbers, with and without regrouping; solve word problems; estimate sums and differences | addends, sum; minuend, subtrahend, difference |
| 3 | Multiplication | multiply by 10, 100, 1000, 10 000; state the properties of multiplication; tables of 11–15; multiply using a grid; multiply 3- and 4-digit numbers; estimate products | multiplicand, multiplier, product; multiplying on a number line |
| 4 | Division | divide by 10, 100, 1000; divide a 3-digit number by a 2-digit number; divide a 4-digit number by 1- and 2-digit numbers; state the properties of division; estimate quotients | division as repeated subtraction; dividend, divisor, quotient; dividing on a number line |
| 5 | Factors and Multiples | find factors and multiples; find common factors and common multiples; tell prime from composite numbers | (starts with arrays: ways to arrange 8 counters in rows and columns) |
| 6 | Fractions | find equivalent fractions; write fractions in simplest form; compare unit and like fractions; add and subtract like fractions; convert mixed ↔ improper fractions | a fraction is an equal part of a whole (halves, quarters, thirds of a pizza) |
| 7 | Decimals | write fractions with denominator 10, 100, 1000 as decimals; write decimals as fractions; read a place-value chart for decimals; draw decimals as pictures | one-tenth = 1/10 = 0.1 ("zero point one", 1 decimal place) |
| 8 | Shapes | tell open from closed figures; identify polygons; measure and draw line segments; draw circles freehand and with compasses; name centre, radius, diameter, chord and arc; make shapes with tangrams; make solids from nets; draw the plan, front view and side view of simple objects | point, line, line segment, ray |
| 9 | Measurement | relate m–cm, cm–mm, m–km; solve length and distance problems; relate units of weight and of volume; convert units of length, weight and volume; add and subtract lengths, weights and volumes; estimate and check; word problems | units: cm, m, km; g, kg; L, mL. 1 m = 100 cm, 1 km = 1000 m, 1 kg = 1000 g, 1 L = 1000 mL |
| 10 | Perimeter and Area | define perimeter and area; find the perimeter of rectangles, squares, triangles and closed figures; find the area of regular and irregular shapes | perimeter = distance around a closed figure |
| 11 | Time | read time to the exact minute; write time in 12-hour and 24-hour clock; find how long an event lasts; days in a year and in each month; why we need leap years; days between two dates; put events in time order | telling time to the hour, half hour and 5 minutes |
| 12 | Money | convert rupees ↔ paise; add, subtract, multiply and divide money; make bills | ₹1 = 100 p (₹180.50 = 18050 p) |
| 13 | Data Handling | collect data; read and interpret bar graphs; show data in a pie chart; draw conclusions from bar graphs and pie charts | reading pictographs with a scale (each ★ = 2 points) |
| 14 | Patterns and Symmetry | find patterns in numbers; say what tiling is; find symmetric figures and their lines of symmetry; complete a symmetric figure from its line of symmetry; draw the mirror image of a figure | growing and shrinking patterns |
