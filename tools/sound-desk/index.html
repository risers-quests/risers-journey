📘 What does each control do?
GAIN (top knob)How strongly the microphone's signal comes into the desk. Set it so the voice is strong but the red CLIP light never comes on.
SIG light (green)Signal is reaching the desk from the stage. If it's off, the problem is before the desk: the mic's switch, its battery or its cable. Send the stage team.
CLIP lightRed means too much signal is coming in and the sound is distorting. Fix it with GAIN, not the fader: the fader comes after the problem.
HI EQTurns the high frequencies up or down. Turning it down makes things softer and darker, and is a quick way to tame a feedback squeal.
MUTESwitches a channel off instantly. Lit red = off. Many "broken mic" emergencies turn out to be a muted channel.
FADERHow much of the channel goes out to the speakers. The "0" line is normal level. Most of a sound engineer's work happens here.
METERThe bar beside the fader shows how loud the channel is right now. Green is healthy. Red is too hot.
Status dot● just right · ▼ too quiet · ▲ too loud, for this service's target mix. In Advanced mode it disappears and you use your ears.
MASTERThe overall volume to the speakers (and your headphones). It doesn't fix the balance between channels.
