# Term 3 — ideas for later (not built)

## Group project board on the Risers' dashboards

Each Riser gets a **Term 3 Quest** section on their own dashboard, showing their group's build.

- **Task board** with four columns: Backlog, To do, Doing, Done. The group adds its own tasks and moves them along.
- **Weekly checkpoints**, the same ones facilitators see on the staff Term 3 page: milestones reached, how it's going (on track / need a hand / stuck), what we finished, blockers, next week's goal and who's doing what.
- **This week's team director enters the updates.** There is no fixed leader: the team picks a director each week, and the turn goes round. At the check-in the facilitator only runs the conversation; the director types it in. The facilitator adds a note and marks the check-in as done.
- **One shared view per group.** Benjamin, Eva and Yokesh all see the same board and checkpoints. When one of them makes a change, the other two see it.
- **The staff Term 3 page** shows the same live data for every group, so facilitators don't enter anything twice.
