Passions

Every Riser gets 1 hour a week for what they love, and grows in it level by level, for as long as they want.

How it works

Step What happens
1. Find it The Riser fills in the Passion Finder and picks their ONE thing
2. Do it 1 hour a week at LifeHub. Time at home counts too
3. Post it In the last 5 minutes, they post their week's work on the Passion Feed
4. Level up When they're ready, their mentor (their own staff) checks and moves them up
5. Get a badge Each level earns a badge

The 7 levels

Same 7 levels for every passion, from a first try to leading others.

Level What it looks like To move up, they show…
1. Spark Trying it out, often 5 posts over at least 3 weeks
2. Kindle Learning the basics from a teacher, course, book or videos One finished piece using what they learned, and who or what they learned from
3. Flame Making their own things, not just copying 3 pieces of their own, and one shown to someone outside LifeHub
4. Blaze Their passion project: something real, with an audience or a purpose A finished project, shared: an exhibition, a stall, a performance, a video, a website, a product
5. Beacon Working with people in the field Feedback from someone outside they worked with or learned from: a visit, a day's shadowing, a real job, a competition
6. Torchbearer Teaching others, leading, winning, selling Taught a workshop, led a team, placed in a competition or sold their work
7. Star Going deeper, for as long as they want A new, bigger goal agreed with their mentor, each time

The heart of it

The Passion Feed

Every week, every Riser shows what they did, and everyone cheers them on.

  • 📸
    Post a photo or a 30-second video, with one line about the week.
  • 🔥
    React with 🔥 ❤️ 👏. Friends and mentors see it.
  • 🎉
    Celebrate: every level-up appears in the feed for everyone.
  • 📈
    Grow: each post fills their level bar.
  • 🛡️
    Kind and safe: the work, not faces. First names only. No comments, counts or rankings. Staff can hide any post.

Already good at it?

They start at the level they've already reached, not at Spark.

Step What happens
1. Show us In their first passion hour, they show their mentor what they can do: their work, a demonstration, certificates, trophies, videos
2. Place The mentor places them at the highest level they can show
3. Badges They get every badge up to that level, marked "earned before"
4. Carry on They post from there, aiming at the next level

Example: a Riser who has played in an academy for 3 years and coaches younger kids can show it, and starts at Torchbearer.

Rule: placement needs proof, not just saying so. Unsure? Place one level lower; they'll move up fast.

Real-world experience

Families tell us what they do for work and whether they'd host a Riser. We match Risers to them.

Step Example
Watch A live match, an exhibition, a performance
Visit An hour at a studio, kitchen, lab or academy
Shadow A day with someone who does it for work
Help Volunteer at a real event
Do A small real job: an order, a stall, a class
Compete An event from Beyond the Classroom

Parent consent and an adult present, always. Staff arrange visits.

Who does what

Who What When
Riser Passion hour; posts their work Weekly
Mentor (their staff) Posting time; level-ups; a 10-minute chat Weekly; chat monthly
One staff member The list of family experts; Beyond the Classroom Monthly
Families Offer visits; help with materials When they can
