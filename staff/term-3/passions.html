Passions

Every Riser gets 1 hour a week for what they love, and grows in it level by level, for as long as they want.

How it works

Step What happens
1. Find it The Riser fills in the Passion Finder and picks their ONE thing
2. Do it 1 hour a week at LifeHub. Time at home counts too
3. Post it In the last 5 minutes, they post their week's work on the Passion Feed
4. Level up When they're ready, their mentor (their own staff) checks and moves them up
5. Get a badge Each level earns a badge

The 7 levels

Level It means… To move up, they show…
1. Spark Trying it out 5 posts over 3 weeks
2. Kindle Learning the basics One thing done well, and who or what they learned from
3. Flame Doing it their own way 3 things of their own; one shown outside LifeHub
4. Blaze Their passion project A real project, finished and shared
5. Beacon Out in the real world A visit, a day with a professional, or a competition
6. Torchbearer Leading others Taught, led, coached or won something
7. Star Going further A new, bigger goal, each time

The Passion Feed

  • Each week: a photo or short video (30 seconds) + one line about their week.
  • Others react: 🔥 ❤️ 👏. No comments.
  • Show the work, not faces. First names only.
  • No counts, no rankings, no streaks.
  • Staff can hide any post.
  • Every post fills their level bar.

Real-world experience

Families tell us what they do for work and whether they'd host a Riser. We match Risers to them.

Step Example
Watch A live match, an exhibition, a performance
Visit An hour at a studio, kitchen, lab or academy
Shadow A day with someone who does it for work
Help Volunteer at a real event
Do A small real job: an order, a stall, a class
Compete An event from Beyond the Classroom

Parent consent and an adult present, always. Staff arrange visits.
