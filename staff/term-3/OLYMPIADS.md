website only**. Each Olympiad below gives one official source. Organisers
can change dates, so check that page again before registering.

question format. Everything else comes from the organiser.

## The big point for LifeHub

Many well-known Olympiads accept students **only through their school**.
Our Risers can enter only the ones that take **individual or homeschooled
students**. Each Olympiad below says which it is.

## The plan

### This term (November 2026 – January 2027)

1. **Indian Talent Olympiad, Slot 2 (4–9 January 2027).** This is a good
   first Olympiad for every Grade 6–8 Riser. Each Riser picks 1–2 subjects
   they like. The organiser hasn't published the Slot 2 registration
   deadline yet, so **ask them this week**.
2. **NSTSE online (24 January 2027).** For Risers who enjoy Maths and
   Science. It gives each Riser a report of their strong and weak areas,
   which is useful for planning.
3. **ZCO (12 December 2026; register by 1 December).** Only for a Riser
   who already writes programs in Python, C++ or Java and enjoys it. For
   anyone else, plan for next year.

### Next year (2027–28)

| When | What | For whom |
|---|---|---|
| Spring 2027 | Ask a partner school, or our NIOS study centre if we have one, whether Risers can sit **SOF** Olympiads through them | All |
| Jun–Jul 2027 | Register for **IOQM** | Grade 8+ Risers who love Maths puzzles (check the birth-date rule) |
| Aug–Sep 2027 | Enrol for **NSEJS** | Grade 8+ Risers born inside that year's window |
| Sep–Nov 2027 | Register for **ZIO/ZCO** | Problem-solvers and coders |

### How to choose who takes what

- **First Olympiad:** start with ITO or NSTSE. Short multiple-choice
  tests at school-syllabus level, taken from home.
- **The national ladders (IOQM and NSEJS):** only for Risers who already
  enjoy hard, unfamiliar problems. Start them on the official past papers
  months ahead. Try one past paper first to see if they enjoy it.
- **Link to passion projects:** a Riser whose passion project is coding
  can aim at ZIO/ZCO. A science-inventor type can aim at INSPIRE-MANAK
  next year (through a partner school).

## Each Olympiad in detail

### Indian Talent Olympiad (ITO): online annual Olympiad

- **What it is:** A yearly multiple-choice Olympiad in Science (ISO), Maths
  (IMO), English (EIO), General Knowledge (GKIO), Computers (ICO) and
  Social Studies (NSSO), plus Drawing and Essay. Its IMO and ISO are **not**
  the same as SOF's or the international ones.
- **Who:** Classes 1–10. Students can register on their own: "Register
  Independently – No school enrollment required."
- **When (2026–27, Round 1, online):** Slot 2: Computers 4 Jan, Social
  Studies 5 Jan, GK 6 Jan, English 7 Jan, Science 8 Jan, Maths 9 Jan 2027,
  between 8 am and 8 pm. The organiser says the dates are tentative and
  the final schedule comes 20 days before. Slot 1 (1–5 Dec 2026) has
  closed: one official page gives its deadline as 30 October, another as
  5 November. The Slot 2 deadline isn't published.
- **Format:** Classes 5–10: 50 questions in 45 minutes. 35 on the subject,
  10 logical reasoning, 5 higher-order thinking. Taken on the ITO app on a
  phone with a front camera.
- **Fee:** ₹500 per subject. Qualifiers pay ₹500 more for Round 2.
- **Skill level:** School syllabus plus reasoning. A good first
  Olympiad.
- **Official source:** https://www.indiantalent.org/online-olympiad-exam-schedule

### NSTSE (National Level Science Talent Search Exam), Unified Council

- **What it is:** A Maths and Science test that "measures how well a
  student has understood the concepts" and gives each student a
  skill-by-skill performance report.
- **Who:** Classes 1–10. "We accept direct/individual applications for
  the NSTSE." The online version is taken at home on a computer with a
  webcam.
- **When (2026–27):** Online for direct students: mock test **17 Jan
  2027**, final exam **24 Jan 2027**. The paper version through schools is
  20 Nov and 2 Dec 2026.
- **Format:** A separate paper for each class. All objective questions, no
  negative marking. Follows the CBSE syllabus, "also suitable" for ICSE
  and state boards.
- **Fee (online):** ₹354, including the performance report, 2 past papers
  and a study e-book.
- **Skill level:** School syllabus, focused on understanding over
  memorising.
- **Official source:** https://www.unifiedcouncil.com/about-nstse-online.html

### Indian Computing Olympiad (IARCS): ZIO, ZCO, then INOI

- **What it is:** Finds school students "with outstanding skills in
  algorithms and computer programming", and chooses India's team for the
  International Olympiad in Informatics.
- **Who:** "Open to all school students… from any school board… There is
  no lower age limit."
- **Stage 1 has two exams; take either or both:**
  - **ZIO**, a written exam (no coding). 21 Nov 2026; registration closed
    6 Nov.
  - **ZCO**, a programming exam in C++, Java or Python. **12 Dec 2026**;
    registration open **till 1 Dec 2026**.
- **After that:** INOI (6 Feb 2027, C++ only), then a training camp of
  about 30 students.
- **Skill level:** Hard. ZCO needs real programming ability. ZIO needs
  logical, step-by-step problem solving.
- **How to prepare:** IARCS's own study material and its archive of past
  problems (both linked from the official page).
- **Official source:** https://www.iarcs.org.in/inoi/

### IOQM: Indian Olympiad Qualifier in Mathematics (stage 1 of the Maths Olympiad)

- **What it is:** The first stage of India's Mathematical Olympiad
  programme. After it: RMO, then INMO, then camps, then the International
  Mathematical Olympiad. It rewards "out-of-the-box thinking and the
  ability to reason logically rather than extensive mathematical
  knowledge".
- **Who:** In 2026: born between 1 Aug 2007 and 31 Jul 2014, **and in
  Class 8–12**. Grades 6–7 can't enter yet. "Students pursuing
  home-schooling or open-schooling are eligible", with supporting
  documents. Students must be eligible to hold an Indian passport, and
  have lived and studied in India for about two years before.
- **How to enter:** Through a registered centre, or individually online.
  Documents: a photo, a school ID or fee receipt or last year's report
  card, and Aadhaar, passport or birth certificate.
- **When:** In 2026, online student registration ran 29 Jun – 27 Jul and
  the exam was on 6 Sep. Expect the same pattern in 2027.
- **Format:** On paper with an OMR sheet, no negative marking.
- **Fee:** ₹300 (₹180 for KV/JNV students).
- **Topics:** Number theory, geometry, algebra and combinatorics. No
  calculus or statistics.
- **Skill level:** Very hard. Non-routine problems.
- **How to prepare:** Past papers on the HBCSE Olympiads site.
- **Official source:** https://ioqm.mtai.org.in/ (eligibility details:
  https://ioqm.mtai.org.in/documents/elcrIOQM.pdf)

### NSEJS: National Standard Examination in Junior Science (stage 1 of the Junior Science Olympiad)

- **What it is:** The first stage towards the International Junior
  Science Olympiad (then INJSO, then a camp). Run by IAPT. The syllabus is
  "broadly equivalent to… Class 10 of CBSE in Science and Mathematics".
  HBCSE says questions are "usually non-conventional and of high
  difficulty level".
- **Who (2026–27):** "Students born between 01 January 2012 and 31
  December 2013, and currently studying in Classes VIII, IX or X". Home-
  and open-schooled students may enter. **The birth-date window moves each
  year, so check it against each Riser's birth date.**
- **How to enter:** "Online Self-Enrolment" or through any registered
  centre.
- **When:** In 2026, individual enrolment ran 21 Aug – 16 Sep, and the
  exam was 22 Nov (2:30–4:30 pm). Expect the same pattern in 2027.
- **Format:** On paper with an OMR sheet, no negative marking.
- **Fee:** ₹300.
- **Skill level:** Very hard. Class 10 science and maths.
- **Official source:** https://iapt.manageexam.com/

### SOF Olympiads (Science Olympiad Foundation): IMO, ISO, IEO and others

- **What it is:** Yearly multiple-choice Olympiads in many subjects.
  - IMO (Maths), Level 1: 50 questions in 60 minutes for Class 5+, in four
    sections: Logical Reasoning, Mathematical Reasoning, Everyday
    Mathematics, and Achievers.
  - Questions come 60% from the current class syllabus and 40% from the
    one before.
- **Who:** Classes 1–12, but "**through their respective schools only.
  Individual registrations are not accepted.**"
- **When (IMO 2026–27):** 23 Oct, 26 Nov or 10 Dec 2026; the school picks
  one. Schools register about 30 days before. Level 2 is for the top
  scorers.
- **Fee:** ₹170 per student per Olympiad (the school may add ₹30).
- **Skill level:** School syllabus plus reasoning.
- **What we'd need:** a partner school, or LifeHub registering as a
  school centre. SOF says "any school can be registered as a center… No
  fee is required."
- **Official source:** https://www.sofworld.org/imo/about-imo-exam/how-to-participate

### INSPIRE-MANAK (Department of Science & Technology): ideas and inventions, not an exam

- **What it is:** For Classes 6–12. Aims to collect original
  "ideas/innovations rooted in science and societal applications".
  Winners can go on to district, state and national exhibitions.
- **Who:** "Schools can nominate 5 best original ideas/innovations of
  students." The school must be registered on the portal.
- **When:** Nominations for 2026–27 closed (last date was 10 Oct 2026).
- **Skill level:** Project-based. A good fit for passion projects.
- **Official source:** https://www.inspireawards-dst.gov.in/
