# Olympiads and competitions for Risers (Grades 6–8)

Researched 9 November 2026. Most dates come from coaching and news sites,
not the organisers themselves, and some sources disagree. **Check each one
on the official site before we register anyone.**

The big catch for LifeHub: many Olympiads only take entries **through a
registered school**, not individual students. Each row below says which
kind it is.

## Open now, or still to come this year

| Olympiad | Subjects | Who can enter | Dates (2026–27) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Indian Talent Olympiad (ITO)** — indiantalent.org | Maths, Science, English, GK, Computers, Drawing, Essay | **Individuals can register online** (Classes 1–12) | Exams through the year; offline school deadline extended to **10 Nov 2026** | The easiest one to do this year. Check the individual deadline today. |
| **SOF Olympiads** — sofworld.org: IMO (Maths), ISO (Science, was NSO), IEO (English) | Maths, Science, English, plus cyber, GK, commerce | **Through a school only** (school picks one date; register 30+ days before) | IMO: 23 Oct, **26 Nov, 10 Dec 2026**. ISO: 30 Oct, **19 Nov, 3 Dec**. IEO: 30 Sep, 27 Oct, **17 Nov**. Level 2: Feb 2027 | Too late for 2026 unless LifeHub is a registered centre or a partner school takes our Risers. Plan for 2027–28. |
| **SilverZone Olympiads** — silverzone.org: iOM, iOS, iOEL, Smart Kid GK, AI Olympiad | Maths, Science, English, GK, AI | Mainly **through a school** | iOM: **18 Nov, 7 Dec**. iOS: **19 Nov, 8 Dec**. iOEL: **26 Nov, 10 Dec**, online 12 Dec. AI Olympiad: 23 Nov / 21 Dec | Ask whether they take individual or centre entries. |

## Not exams, but worth knowing (still to look up)

These suit passion projects better than tests. Dates not checked yet:

- **INSPIRE-MANAK** (Govt of India): Classes 6–10 send an idea or innovation; it's nominated through a school.
- **IRIS National Fair**: science research projects (usually Class 5 and up).
- **Homi Bhabha Bal Vaidnyanik** (Maharashtra): Classes 6 and 9.
- **World Robot Olympiad / Technoxian**: robotics teams.
- **Hindustan Olympiad**: nothing found yet; search again.
