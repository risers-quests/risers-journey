# Passion projects: a system to brainstorm (not built yet)

Step 1 is built: the **Passion Finder** questionnaire (Term 3 →
Passion Finder). Staff OK it and choose which Risers see it, then read
their answers. This note is for deciding what happens **after** that:
how a Riser turns an idea into a project and grows it into a real skill.

## The journey, step by step (proposal)

1. **Find:** the Riser fills in the Passion Finder.
2. **Talk:** a 15-minute chat with a facilitator. Together they pick
   **one** project and the skill it builds.
3. **Project card:** the Riser fills in a one-page card: the project
   name, the skill, why it matters to them, what "done" looks like, and
   how they'll show it (exhibition, video, selling it, teaching a class,
   a competition…). A facilitator OKs it, the same way they OK an Own Quest.
4. **Skill ladder:** the skill split into 4–5 levels, from *Beginner* to
   *Can teach it*. Each level has one thing the Riser can show. For
   example, for baking: "follow a recipe", "change a recipe", "invent a
   recipe", "bake to order", "run a stall".
5. **Milestones:** 3–6 steps across the term, each with a date.
6. **Weekly log:** each week, 3 questions, 2 minutes: *What did I do?
   What did I learn? What's next / where am I stuck?* Plus a photo or link.
7. **Mentor check-ins:** every 2–3 weeks with a facilitator, or with an
   outside mentor (a parent or someone who knows the skill). They move
   the Riser up the ladder when they've shown the level.
8. **Showcase:** at the end of term, each Riser shows their project to
   families. Their portfolio collects the photos and log.
9. **Look back, and go on or change:** keep growing the same skill next
   term, or choose a new project.

## Questions to decide together

- **Time:** how many hours a week? Fixed slots in the timetable (e.g. two
  "Passion hours"), or at home with check-ins at LifeHub?
- **Solo or in teams?** Solo projects with "skill buddies" who share a
  topic, or small teams like the Quests?
- **Who mentors?** Facilitators only, parents, or outside experts
  (one session a month)?
- **How long?** One term, or a year-long project with a term checkpoint?
- **Recognition:** badges for each ladder level, a certificate, a line in
  the End-of-Term report, a portfolio for the
  [10th grade / other path](../../term-3/grade10/) conversation, or a
  competition entry (see [OLYMPIADS.md](OLYMPIADS.md)).
- **Money and materials:** a small budget per Riser? Who buys things?
- **Changing your mind:** when can a Riser drop a project, and what do they
  do first (e.g. a short "what I learned" note)?

## What we could build in the app (once decided)

- **Riser page "My Passion Project":** the project card, skill ladder,
  milestones and weekly log, saved through `/sync` like everything else
  (one record per Riser, e.g. week key `term3-passion-project`).
- **Staff board:** every Riser's project, level, last log entry and next
  check-in at a glance. "Stuck" or "no log for 2 weeks" flagged.
- **Skill ladder library:** ready-made ladders for common skills (coding,
  drawing, baking, video, music, writing, robotics, small business) that
  facilitators can copy and change.
- **Showcase page:** photos and a short write-up per Riser for families.
- **Reports:** the ladder level shown in the End-of-Term report.

Suggested first build: the project card + weekly log + staff board. Ladders
and showcase can come next.
